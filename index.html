About Me

The Story Behind
The Music

Born between two worlds and raised on raw emotion, I've spent the last decade turning life's contradictions into music people feel in their chest. My sound pulls from jazz, neo-soul, and cinematic folk — but really it lives somewhere unnamed, somewhere honest.

I've toured across 28 countries, released three studio albums, and collaborated with artists I once had on my bedroom wall as a teenager. None of that matters as much as what happens when the lights go low and the first chord rings out.
